WHERE IT ALL STARTED

A Country That Became Part Of The Story

✦

Namibia is a land of vast open spaces, unforgettable landscapes and horizons that seem to stretch forever. It is a place where adventure is never far away and every journey begins with the promise of something waiting beyond the next horizon.

From dramatic desert landscapes to long open roads, Namibia has always carried a sense of freedom, exploration and possibility. It is one of the places that forms an important part of the story behind a life lived across countries, continents and cultures.

Every journey has a beginning, and every place leaves its mark. Namibia remains an unforgettable part of the story and a reminder that sometimes the greatest adventures begin long before we realise where the road will eventually take us.
