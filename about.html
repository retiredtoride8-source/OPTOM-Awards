OUR STORY

Two Motorcycles. One Meeting. A Lifetime of Adventure.

The Ride Never Gets Old

The Retired to Ride Touring Awards were created to celebrate motorcycle tourers aged 60 and over who continue to chase adventure, explore the world and inspire others through their journeys.

Retirement is not the end of the road. For many riders, it is the beginning of a new adventure.

These awards honour those who prove that passion, curiosity and the freedom of the open road never disappear.

How It All Began

The Retired to Ride story began on 12 January 2014 in New Zealand.

JR arrived on his BMW motorcycle. Brenda arrived on her Can-Am.

Two riders, two motorcycles, and one chance meeting that would become the beginning of an incredible journey together.

Meet The Honeybadger

Known throughout the Retired to Ride community as "The Honeybadger", Brenda has become a much-loved personality among followers of their adventures.

The nickname represents her spirit perfectly: strong, determined, adventurous and always ready for the next road.

From New Zealand To The World

What started in New Zealand grew into a worldwide motorcycle adventure.

Today JR and Brenda call Spain home, but their journey continues across roads and countries throughout Europe and beyond.

Their travels have included Spain, France, Germany, Belgium, the Netherlands, Denmark and England, with many more destinations still waiting to be discovered.

Why The Touring Awards Exist

During their travels, JR and The Honeybadger met many incredible riders whose stories deserved to be recognised.

Riders who continued exploring. Riders who inspired others. Riders who proved that age is never a reason to stop chasing adventure.

The Retired to Ride Touring Awards were created to celebrate these riders and the worldwide community they represent.

Welcome To The Community

This website is the home of the Retired to Ride community.

A place to celebrate adventures, music, friendships and the riders who continue proving that the road never gets old.
