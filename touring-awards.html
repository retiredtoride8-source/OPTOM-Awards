Celebrating Riders Who Prove The Ride Never Gets Old

Honouring motorcycle tourers aged 60 and over who continue to inspire through adventure, friendship and passion.

Why These Awards Exist

Motorcycle touring is more than the distance travelled. It is about the people met along the way, the memories created, and the stories shared.

The Retired to Ride Touring Awards were created to recognise riders aged 60 and over who continue to explore the world, embrace adventure and inspire others through their journeys.

These riders prove that retirement is not the end of the road. For many, it is where the greatest adventures begin.

What We Celebrate

The Retired to Ride Touring Awards celebrate riders who represent the true spirit of motorcycle touring.

🏍️ Adventure and exploration

🌍 Long-distance touring achievements

🤝 Friendship and community

❤️ Inspiration to other riders

⭐ A lifelong passion for motorcycles

The Spirit Behind The Award

This award is not only about miles travelled or destinations reached.

It is about the courage to keep exploring, the friendships built along the road and the belief that adventure has no age limit.

Every rider has a story worth celebrating.
