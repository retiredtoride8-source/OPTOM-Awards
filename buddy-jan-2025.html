Returning To Europe

After their unforgettable 2024 adventure through Spain and Andorra, Buddy and Jan decided the European roads were calling them back.

In 2025, they once again flew from the United States to Spain, this time beginning their journey in Madrid.

Exploring Southern Spain

Their journey took them through some of Spain's most spectacular riding areas.

🇪🇸 Madrid

🏔 Ronda

⛰ Sierra Nevada

From dramatic mountain roads to beautiful landscapes, Buddy and Jan continued creating memories together on two wheels.

Why Buddy & Jan Were Chosen Again

The Retired to Ride Touring Awards recognise more than distance travelled.

They celebrate passion, courage and the willingness to keep exploring new roads.

Buddy and Jan returned to Europe because one adventure was not enough.

They showed that curiosity has no age limit and that the greatest journeys are often the ones still waiting ahead.

Two Years. Two Adventures. One Passion.

From their first European motorcycle experience in 2024 to their return journey in 2025, Buddy and Jan represent everything the Retired to Ride community celebrates.

Adventure. Friendship. Exploration. The freedom of the open road.
